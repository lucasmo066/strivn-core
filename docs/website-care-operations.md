## Provisional operating budget

Essential is $225 quarterly / $810 annually, including 30 minutes of small edits per quarter. Priority Care remains $675 quarterly / $2,430 annually with 3 total editing hours per quarter and a quarterly review. Both cover one standard business site's hosting-provider costs up to $25 per calendar month, with no rollover or cash credit.

For initial planning, reserve the entire $20 Pro platform fee for the first managed site, with $5 remaining within its hosting allowance for variable hosting costs. This is a conservative allocation, not proof of what that site will cost or a guarantee that usage remains below $25. Credits offset eligible usage; do not add the $20 credit as another subscription charge or count it once per project. On a shared account, document an allocation of platform/seat costs before onboarding and track project-level usage. Allocate shared credits consistently; never charge a shared base fee in full to every client and describe that as actual cost.

## Before onboarding a managed site

1. Verify the actual team plan, deploying seats, paid add-ons, latest full invoice and project usage after team authorization. Check October 2026 as a full month and November month-to-date separately. Reconcile team charges to invoices; usage-charge exports may omit subscriptions, tax, or external services.
2. Record hosting and external-service estimates in the scope. Confirm the site fits the allowance; quote heavier applications, media delivery, custom systems, or dedicated hosting separately.
3. Set appropriate project usage alerts where supported and review monthly. The contractual allowance is not a configured provider spending cap. No billing or alert settings have been changed as part of the website copy update.
4. Request written approval for higher costs or an upgrade before exceeding the allowance. Strivn bears unapproved provider overages; never surprise-invoice the client. Agree on optimization or a higher-capacity arrangement in advance.
5. Record editing time separately from routine operations. No automatic rollover. Do not promise unlimited repairs or completion SLAs.

## Renewal and handoff

Confirm the shared CARE_TERMS wording in the signed service agreement. Initial care price is protected for 12 months; subsequent changes apply at renewal with at least 30 days' written notice. Clients can cancel the next period by emailing before renewal; prepaid service runs through the paid-through date, without pro-rata refunds except as required by their agreement or law.

Include one standard handoff of the existing project, agreed files and access to compatible client-paid hosting when requested before the paid-through date. Quote rebuilds or migrations requiring extra development. Agree on the transfer date, identify non-transferable licenses/secrets and vendor accounts, preserve domain ownership, and confirm the destination works before disconnecting Strivn-paid hosting. Included hosting ends at the paid-through date; communicate that a site not transferred can go offline. No automatic third-party cancellations. Do not alter existing client agreements through website copy.
